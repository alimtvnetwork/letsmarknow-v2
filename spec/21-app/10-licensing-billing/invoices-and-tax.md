# Invoices & Tax

Invoice generation, tax handling, and receipt delivery.

---

## 1. Source of truth

- Invoices generated by the processor (Stripe / Paddle).
- We **mirror** invoice metadata into our DB for the Billing UI; we never generate our own.
- PDF download links proxied through our API for auth + audit.

## 3. Tax handling

### Stripe Tax
- `automatic_tax.enabled = true` on subscriptions.
- Tax registered in jurisdictions where we have nexus (US states, etc.).
- B2B reverse-charge applied automatically when tax ID present.

### Paddle (MoR)
- Paddle is the seller of record for EU/UK/AU/NZ.
- We receive net amounts; Paddle remits all VAT/GST.
- Customers see Paddle as merchant on their bank statement.

### Display
- B2C prices shown inc-tax (with breakdown on invoice).
- B2B prices shown ex-tax.
- Toggle in `/settings/billing`.

## 4. Invoice numbering

- Stripe: `LMN-{YYYY}-{seq}` enforced via custom invoice number suffix.
- Paddle: their numbering used; mirrored as-is.
- Sequence per Org (continuous; no gaps).

## 5. Receipt email

- Auto-sent by processor on invoice paid.
- We additionally send a branded "Thanks for your payment" with link to `/settings/billing/invoices`.
- Configurable: Owner can disable our duplicate (defaults to on).

## 6. Tax IDs

- Collected at Checkout (`tax_id_collection.enabled=true`).
- Editable later in Customer Portal.
- Validated by processor (VIES for EU, etc.).
- Stored on `org`; displayed on every invoice.

## 7. Billing details

- Company name, billing address, tax ID maintained per Org.
- Editable in `/settings/billing/details`.
- Changes apply to NEXT invoice; past invoices immutable.

## 8. Invoice viewing

`/settings/billing/invoices`:
- Table sorted by `issued_at desc`.
- Columns: number, period, amount, status, PDF download.
- Filters by year, status.
- CSV export of all invoices for accounting.

## 9. PDF access

- `GET /v1/billing/invoices/:id/pdf` → 302 to processor's signed URL.
- Auth required (Owner / Billing role).
- Audit log entry per download.

## 10. Telemetry

- `invoice.created` `{ amount_minor, currency, has_tax_id }`
- `invoice.paid` `{ amount_minor, currency }`
- `invoice.downloaded` `{ format }`
- `invoice.tax_calculated` `{ rate, country }`

## 11. Compliance

- VAT MOSS / OSS handled by processor for EU.
- US sales tax: Stripe Tax registers + remits via filing partner.
- Receipts kept 7 years (legal); processor handles primary retention; we mirror as long as Org exists + 1 year after deletion.

## 12. Edge cases

| Case | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Invoice for canceled subscription | Final invoice generated; org_subscription transitions to `canceled` |
| Tax ID added mid-cycle | Applied to next invoice; current invoice unchanged |
| Refund issued | New `credit_note` mirrored; original invoice unchanged |
| Currency change (rare) | New invoices in new currency; historical retained |
| Plan grandfathered | Invoice carries grandfathered price line items |
